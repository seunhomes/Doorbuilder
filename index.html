The Process

Twelve weeks,
one door, two hands.

In the shop · Sterling Rd.
  1. 01

    You design

    Use the configurator to choose silhouette, material, hardware and glass. Save and share the link with your architect or partner — no signup needed.

  2. 02

    We measure

    A site visit anywhere in the GTA. We document the rough opening, jamb depth, floor pitch and prevailing light — every door is built to its exact opening.

  3. 03

    We mill

    Hardwood is rough-cut, kiln-stabilised, then milled to spec. Stiles and rails are joined with traditional mortise-and-tenon. No MDF, no veneer, no shortcuts.

  4. 04

    We finish & hang

    Eight coats of conversion varnish, sanded between each. Hardware is dry-fit, hinges mortised, and the door is hung by our install crew with a 25-year warranty.

Material Sourcing

Wood with a
paper trail.

Every species is FSC-certified and traceable to the forest of harvest. We work exclusively with mills within a 600-km radius — most of our hardwood is felled in the Algonquin and Frontenac highlands.

White Oak

Frontenac, ON · 280 km

Tight grain, ammonia-fumed for warmth. Our most popular hardwood — it dents less than walnut and ages to honey.

Walnut

Niagara, ON · 130 km

Dramatic chocolate heartwood with violet undertones. We hand-select boards for figured grain on premium commissions.

Cherry

Muskoka, ON · 220 km

Smooth, fine-grained, and famously photochromic — cherry deepens from blush pink to rich auburn over its first decade.

Hard Maple

Algonquin, ON · 290 km

The lightest of our hardwoods, with a near-invisible grain. Reads almost ceramic when finished with a matte topcoat.
